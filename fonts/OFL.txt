Fonts in this directory are self-hosted because the site's Content Security
Policy sets `font-src 'self'` - a CDN or Google Fonts <link> would be blocked.

Both files are the variable (300-700) latin and latin-ext subsets as served by
Google Fonts. To refresh them, re-download the woff2 URLs from
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Space+Grotesk:wght@300..700 and
https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Geist:wght@300..700 with a modern
browser user-agent, and keep the unicode-range declarations in styles.css in
sync.
